Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Status Manager ("the app"), provided by Bansode Tech Solution ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account. We do not collect or send your personal information, app usage data, or status media to our servers. The app does not include advertising or analytics.

Status media and device permissions

If you grant the relevant Android photo, video, or file access, the app uses it to find and display status media available on your device. Media you choose to save is saved on your device. This content is processed locally by the app and is not uploaded to us.

You can manage or revoke the app's permissions in your Android settings. If you share content from the app using another app, that content will be handled according to the recipient app's own privacy practices.

Storage and deletion

We do not keep your personal information or status media on our servers. Status media you save may remain in your device storage until you delete it. You can delete saved files using the app, where available, or your device's file manager. Uninstalling the app may not remove files you previously saved outside the app's private storage.

Third-party services

The app does not use advertising or analytics services. Android and Google Play provide the device and app distribution platform and may process information under their own privacy policies. WhatsApp is a service of Meta; Status Manager is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from children or other users. If you have a concern about information associated with the app, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page, with its effective date updated above.
