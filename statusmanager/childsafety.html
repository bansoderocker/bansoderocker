Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Bansode Tech Solution is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation. These standards apply to Status Manager and anyone using it.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and child sexual exploitation. No one may use Status Manager to create, request, access, possess, save, promote, or share CSAM, or to groom, exploit, sexualize, or otherwise endanger a child. This includes attempts to facilitate or encourage such conduct.

How Status Manager handles content

Status Manager is a local device utility for viewing and saving WhatsApp status media. It does not provide public profiles, user-to-user messaging, a content-hosting service, or a way to upload media to our servers. The app processes status media on the user's device; we cannot remove or moderate content hosted by WhatsApp or stored on a user's device.

Users must not use the app to obtain, save, or redistribute material that sexually exploits or endangers a child. Do not download, copy, forward, or attach suspected CSAM to a report.

Report a child-safety concern

To report suspected child sexual exploitation, CSAM, grooming, or another child-safety concern involving Status Manager, email bansode.vijay1996@gmail.com with the subject "Child safety report". Include a description of the concern and any relevant dates or context. Do not include or send explicit images or videos.

For content or conduct on WhatsApp, use WhatsApp's in-app reporting tools as well. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate child-protection authority immediately.

How reports are handled

We review child-safety reports promptly and prioritize reports involving suspected CSAM or immediate risk to a child. We assess whether the reported activity involves a feature or service we control, may request non-explicit clarifying details, and take appropriate action. We limit access to report details to people handling the report and retain information only as needed to review it, protect safety, and meet legal obligations.

Because Status Manager does not host or receive status media, we cannot remove content from WhatsApp or remotely delete files from a user's device. Where an issue involves functionality or distribution we control, we may disable the relevant feature, restrict access, or remove or suspend the app as appropriate.

Enforcement and cooperation with authorities

We prohibit use of Status Manager to facilitate child sexual abuse or exploitation. We may investigate credible reports, take action within our control, and report suspected illegal conduct to relevant authorities where required or appropriate. We cooperate with lawful requests from law-enforcement and child-protection authorities in accordance with applicable law. We do not promise access to content or records that we do not possess.

For child-safety reports or questions about these standards, contact Bansode Tech Solution at bansode.vijay1996@gmail.com.
